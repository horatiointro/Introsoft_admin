# ALTIL Acceptable Use and AI Safety Policy

**Draft for operational and legal approval.** Applies to tenants, users, applications, API keys and content submitted to ALTIL.

## Allowed use
Use ALTIL only for lawful business, research, accessibility and personal productivity purposes permitted by the selected model, order and local law. Keep a human accountable for consequential decisions. Test outputs before production use and provide required disclosures when people interact with AI.

## Prohibited use
Do not use ALTIL to facilitate violence, weapons, terrorism, child sexual abuse, exploitation, stalking, unlawful discrimination, fraud, phishing, credential theft, malware, unauthorised access, privacy invasion, non-consensual intimate imagery, impersonation, deceptive manipulation, or evasion of safety controls. Do not generate or distribute illegal content, expose another tenant’s data, scrape or spam, or process sensitive personal information without a lawful basis and appropriate safeguards.

Do not rely on model output alone for medical diagnosis or treatment, legal advice, credit/insurance/employment/housing eligibility, education admission, policing, biometric identification, safety-critical operation, or other high-impact determinations. These uses require explicit written approval, documented impact assessment, human review, auditability, and compliance with applicable law. No approval makes an unlawful use permissible.

## Customer controls
Customer must assess its use case, obtain necessary consent/rights, minimise personal data, configure policies, review outputs, secure keys, restrict access, monitor activity, keep an incident contact, and promptly report abuse or compromise. Do not include passwords, payment-card data, authentication secrets or unnecessary sensitive data in prompts. Customer must ensure all users and downstream users are bound by these rules.

## Enforcement
ALTIL may screen, redact, refuse, rate-limit, quarantine, suspend a key or tenant, preserve evidence, notify the account owner, and report credible unlawful conduct where legally required. Controls are risk-based and may produce false positives/negatives. Request review through the support contact; urgent safety/security action may precede review. Repeated or severe abuse may result in termination, subject to law and contract.

## Reporting
Report suspected policy abuse, vulnerable-person risk, data exposure or security issues through **[Introsoft abuse/security contact to be completed]**, including tenant, request ID, time, and relevant evidence. Do not include additional sensitive content unless necessary.
