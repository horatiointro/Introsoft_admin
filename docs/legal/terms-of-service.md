## 4. Orders, packages and licence limits
Each order identifies tenant hierarchy, applications/scopes, package, billing currency, billing period, included quota, overage rate, threshold, usage definition, support, taxes and any negotiated discount. Package quotas are measured by successful billable requests as specified in the order and usage schedule. Retries, provider fallback and partial failures are metered only as the order states. Failed, blocked, refused or provider-error calls are not intentionally billed as successful inference; billing records and request IDs are available for review. No quota may be pooled between licences unless the order permits it. Unused included units expire at period end unless the order says otherwise.

## 5. Usage guardrails and suspension
Customer must configure and maintain its own content, safety, privacy and access controls in addition to ALTIL’s selected platform guardrails. Policy screening applies to routed requests, subject to the chosen configuration and technical limits. We may block a request, disable a key/model, throttle, place a licence in read-only state, or suspend a tenant to enforce safety, quota, security, payment or legal requirements. At a hard quota, routing stops for the affected licence until reset, authorised top-up, plan change or support action. We will return a clear status and reset/next-action information where technically possible. Emergency controls may be immediate; other material suspension reasons will be communicated and reviewed promptly.
