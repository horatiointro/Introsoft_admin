# ALTIL Billing, Payments, Refunds and Reconciliation Policy

**Draft for Finance, payment-provider and legal approval.** The order form and invoice control the customer-specific commercial terms; this policy explains the intended billing operations. Complete Introsoft identity, tax registration, price book, collection/retry days, threshold settings and contacts before publishing.

## Prices, currencies and tax
Prices and package quotas are displayed before purchase in the order currency. Amounts are exclusive of taxes unless clearly labelled inclusive. VAT or other taxes are charged where applicable; valid exemption evidence must be supplied before invoicing. Currency conversion, if agreed, uses the disclosed rate/source/date in the invoice. Discounts apply only to the stated term, scope and volume and do not stack unless documented.

## Billing methods
ALTIL may support invoice/manual settlement, hosted one-time checkout, recurring subscriptions, prepaid credits, usage arrears, threshold-triggered top-ups, and enterprise purchase orders. Available methods depend on country, currency, gateway capability, risk checks and customer mandate. A payment schedule shows fixed period, usage-based trigger, threshold, minimum charge, cap, retry behavior and notice settings. Threshold collection is attempted only against a valid stored gateway token/mandate and never via raw stored card or bank credentials.

## Usage and invoice lifecycle
Usage is recorded against tenant, application, licence and API key with timestamp, request ID, model/provider, billable units, price-book version, guardrail outcome and result state. Invoice lifecycle: draft calculation → review/approval → issue with immutable number and itemised lines → send → payment/credit allocation → partial/paid/overdue → dispute/adjustment/credit note/refund/write-off → closed. Invoice changes after issue use credit/debit notes, never silent mutation. Each payment maps to a gateway event and invoice allocation; fees and settlement amounts are separate accounting lines.

## Collection and suspension
Card attempts are performed by the selected gateway. On decline or timeout, the attempt remains pending/failed and no payment is recorded until a verified success event. Retries follow disclosed intervals and provider rules; customers receive notices and can update payment method. A threshold/top-up balance can pause further automatic charges at a configured cap. An overdue undisputed balance may trigger graduated warnings, restricted mode and suspension after the stated grace period and required notice. Hard request quotas stop new billable AI calls at the configured limit. Customer portal reports the reason, balance/usage, reset date and restoration path. Payment or credit must be reconciled before reactivation where the gateway event is authoritative.

## Currency, display and exchange rates
ALTIL currently meters AI usage and maintains its platform billing and management reporting base in USD. A signed offer, issued invoice, payment, refund or journal keeps the currency explicitly recorded on that source transaction. A tenant may select a local display currency in its portal; converted portal values are estimates for readability and do not amend a contract, invoice, payment instruction or ledger entry. Conversion is shown only when an administrator has configured a rate with a named source and timestamp inside the configured freshness window. If a rate is absent or stale, ALTIL displays the original amount and currency. Rate changes do not rewrite historical records or automatically post foreign-exchange gains/losses. Introsoft's finance owner must approve FX accounting, tax, settlement and close treatment before production use.

## Payment methods and mandates
Hosted gateways collect payment details. ALTIL stores provider customer/payment-method references or vaulted tokens only when needed, encrypted and access restricted; it never stores CVV and is not a card vault. Saved-card and bank debit mandates state payee, amount basis, frequency/trigger, cancellation method and notice. Customer can cancel a mandate or subscription in the portal, subject to accrued fees and the order. A mandate does not authorise unspecified charges. Changes to price, threshold or cadence require advance notice and fresh consent where required.

## Refunds and cancellation
Request a refund through the billing portal or **[billing contact]**, quoting invoice, payment and reason. Introsoft reviews duplicate, erroneous, unauthorised, service-not-delivered, statutory cooling-off and discretionary requests promptly. Unused prepaid balances are reviewed on cancellation and refunded where required by law or order, less lawful, disclosed amounts actually owed. Consumed usage, completed one-time services and provider processing fees are not automatically refundable; this never removes non-waivable statutory rights. Approved refunds return to the original source where possible. Gateway processing time is outside Introsoft’s control. Subscription cancellation prevents future cycles but does not erase accrued usage or current-term obligations except where law/order says otherwise.

## Charge disputes and credits
Raise invoice disputes within 30 days of issue with line-level evidence; pay undisputed amounts by due date. ALTIL acknowledges and investigates with usage logs, price book and gateway records. Credits, refunds, write-offs and tax corrections require reason, source document, approver and immutable audit event. Refunds may not exceed captured net amount less prior refunds. A chargeback is investigated with gateway evidence and does not itself alter ledger balances until the provider event is verified.

## Accounting and settlement
Books use double-entry journal lines in a configured functional currency with transaction currency preserved. Core mappings: issued invoice—Dr Accounts Receivable, Cr AI Service Revenue, Cr Tax Payable; gateway capture—Dr Gateway Clearing, Cr Accounts Receivable; settlement—Dr Bank, Dr Merchant Fees, Cr Gateway Clearing; credit note—Dr Contra-Revenue/Tax Payable, Cr Accounts Receivable; refund—Dr Refunds Payable/Customer Credit, Cr Gateway Clearing or Bank. These are policy mappings, not tax/accounting advice; Introsoft’s accountant must confirm account codes, IFRS/GAAP basis, tax timing and revenue recognition. Subledgers must reconcile to control accounts, bank/provider settlements and tenant statements.

## Settlement matching and close
Gateway events are verified, deduplicated by immutable provider event/transaction ID, matched to payment attempt, tenant, invoice, gross, fee, net, currency and settlement batch. Unmatched, duplicate, short/over, chargeback and stale pending items enter an exception queue. Finance records match decisions, evidence and approver; no manual override erases provider payload or audit history. Daily gateway reconciliation and periodic bank reconciliation are recommended; month close records cut-off, FX, accrued usage, tax, deferred/prepaid revenue, fees, refunds, receivables ageing, journal review and locked-period adjustments. This application is an operational subledger, not a substitute for statutory general-ledger, tax, or audited financial statements until integrated and approved by Introsoft’s finance professional.

## Gateway capabilities
Stripe can support hosted checkout, recurring billing and stored payment methods subject to account enablement and mandate consent. PayFast supports hosted payment and recurring subscription/tokenisation flows; recurring token charges are card-based and require the provider mandate/token and passphrase-protected API. iKhokha iK Pay API supports hosted payment links with signed callbacks and transaction-history reconciliation; do not assume tokenised recurring debits are available unless iKhokha separately enables and documents them for Introsoft. Gateway onboarding, merchant approval, settlement accounts, fees and live credentials are Introsoft’s responsibility.

Keep invoices, credits, gateway payload references, reconciliations, approvals and journals for the applicable statutory/accounting period under the retention schedule.
