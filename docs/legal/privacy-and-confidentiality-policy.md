# ALTIL Privacy, Data Processing and Confidentiality Policy

**Draft for privacy-officer and legal review.** This customer-facing notice describes the intended service posture; the signed data-processing agreement and tenant configuration control where applicable. Introsoft must complete its registered identity, Information Officer, contact, retention schedule, subprocessors and transfer details before publication.

## Roles and scope
For account administration, billing, fraud prevention, security and service analytics, Introsoft generally determines purposes and means as a responsible party/controller. For tenant prompts, files, outputs, knowledge bases, end-user records and messages processed on a tenant’s instructions, the tenant generally determines the purpose and Introsoft acts as operator/processor, subject to the facts and law. Payment providers independently process payer data under their own notices. Actual roles must be confirmed per use case.

## Information handled
We may process tenant identity and organisation hierarchy; account contacts, users and access/audit events; API-key identifiers and hashed secrets; prompts and responses; model/provider, token, latency and error metadata; policy decisions; support communications; device identifiers and encrypted push tokens; message delivery metadata; invoices, usage, tax, payment references and reconciliation data; and security/diagnostic logs. Do not send full card numbers, CVV, bank credentials or unnecessary special-category/sensitive personal information through prompts or support channels.

## Purposes and lawful basis
Purposes include providing and securing ALTIL, routing requests, enforcing guardrails and quotas, metering, billing and collections, support, abuse prevention, legal compliance, service reliability, incident response and (where permitted) product analytics. The tenant is responsible for a lawful basis, notices and permissions for personal information it submits. Introsoft’s own account/billing processing relies on the applicable contract, legal obligations, legitimate interests and consent where required.

## Models and subprocessors
Prompts may be sent to configured AI, cloud, email, push and payment providers as required to provide the selected feature. The exact provider, region, retention and training treatment can vary by route and change over time. ALTIL must expose the current subprocessor/model schedule and tenant controls before production use. Providers are contractually restricted where available; Customer should avoid confidential or personal data unless the selected route and agreement support it.

## Confidentiality
Each party protects non-public information using reasonable safeguards, limits access to personnel/subprocessors who need it and are bound by confidentiality, and uses it only for the service, support, security or lawful compliance. Confidentiality does not cover information independently known, public without breach, lawfully received without restriction, or independently developed. Legally compelled disclosure will be limited and notified when lawful. Customer content is not sold or used for unrelated advertising.

## Security and incidents
ALTIL is designed for tenant scoping, role controls, encrypted provider/device secrets, key hashing, policy enforcement, audit trails and payment tokenisation through gateways. Security depends on deployment configuration, key management, network protection, backups, patching and administrator practices. Customer secures its endpoints, users and API keys. Each party reports suspected breaches promptly; Introsoft will investigate, contain, preserve evidence, cooperate and make legally required regulator/data-subject notifications within the applicable statutory period.

## Retention, access and deletion
Operational records are retained only for documented service, security, dispute, tax and legal purposes. Prompt/output retention, backups, deletion deadlines, data residency, export format and legal holds must be configured in the tenant schedule and operational retention register before launch; deletion from backups may lag until their normal expiry. Customer may request access, correction, export, restriction or deletion through **[privacy contact]**; requests are verified and handled within applicable legal deadlines. Some accounting/security records may need to be retained by law.

## International transfers and children
Cross-border processing may occur through selected providers. Introsoft must identify locations and implement an applicable lawful transfer mechanism and safeguards before enabling such routes. ALTIL is not designed for children’s use; tenants must not knowingly submit children’s personal information except under a documented, lawful, specifically approved use case.

## Data subject rights and complaints
Requests should first be directed to the tenant that determined the purpose; Introsoft assists the tenant as required under the DPA. For Introsoft’s own account data, contact **[Information Officer email/address]**. South African data subjects may complain to the Information Regulator at its current official contact channel. Retain proof of requests and responses.

## Confidential information and AI
Treat prompts, source code, trade secrets and outputs as confidential when marked or reasonably understood to be so. Do not submit another party’s confidential content without authority. AI output can be inaccurate or reveal memorised information; review before disclosure or reliance. The tenant is responsible for rights and permissions in its content and downstream disclosures.
