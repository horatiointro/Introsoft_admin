# ALTIL Refund and Cancellation Policy

This policy is a customer-friendly summary and must be read with the order, Billing Policy and mandatory law. **Draft for Introsoft approval.**

1. **Cancel renewal:** Tenant billing admins can request cancellation in the portal or by written notice to **[billing email]**. The confirmation shows the effective date. Access normally continues through a paid period unless security, law, non-payment or the order requires a restriction. Cancellation stops future recurring charges after the effective date; it does not automatically cancel usage already incurred.
2. **One-time/usage charges:** Verified successful usage, completed service and charges properly disclosed at checkout are generally non-refundable. Duplicate, incorrectly calculated, unauthorised, unavailable-service and legally protected transactions are investigated and corrected where substantiated.
3. **Prepaid balance:** On termination, remaining paid credits are reviewed and refunded where required by law/order; promotional or expired credits may not be cash refundable where law permits. Valid outstanding fees, credits, chargebacks and taxes are settled through an itemised final statement.
4. **Subscriptions:** Cancellation prevents the next renewal after any disclosed notice period. Any fixed-term commitment, pro-rata treatment or early-cancellation amount must have been clearly disclosed in the order and remain lawful. No undisclosed penalty applies.
5. **How to request:** Include tenant name, invoice number, gateway transaction/payment reference, amount, date, and reason. Do not email full card or bank details. Introsoft will acknowledge within five business days, investigate, and communicate the result and any evidence needed. Approved refunds are submitted to the original provider/method where possible; gateway/bank posting times vary.
6. **Statutory rights:** Nothing here excludes a right that cannot lawfully be excluded, including any applicable consumer, electronic transaction or direct-marketing cancellation rights. Eligibility and time periods depend on customer status, transaction channel, service commencement, consent and applicable law.
7. **Disputes:** Contact **[billing contact]** first for an invoice review. This does not prevent contacting a payment provider, card issuer, regulator, ombud or court with jurisdiction.
